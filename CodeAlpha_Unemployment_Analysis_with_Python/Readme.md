An Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) project evaluating India's unemployment trends, regional (state-level) impacts, urban-rural disparities, and economic shocks during the **2020 COVID-19 lockdown**. Created for the **CodeAlpha Data Science Internship Program**[cite: 1].

---

## 📌 Project Overview
* Cleaned and preprocessed nationwide monthly employment records from **May 2019 to June 2020**[cite: 1].
* Evaluated seasonal variations and state-level spikes in unemployment rates[cite: 1].
* Investigated urban vs. rural economic resilience during COVID-19 pandemic restrictions[cite: 1].
* Identified key patterns to inform labor and socio-economic policy discussions[cite: 1].

---

## 📊 Dataset Description
The dataset (`Unemployment in India.csv`) contains 740 cleaned records spanning 28 Indian States and Union Territories[cite: 1]:
* **Region:** State or Union Territory name
* **Date:** Monthly reporting dates (May 2019 – June 2020)
* **Area:** Categorical classification (`Urban` vs. `Rural`)
* **Estimated Unemployment Rate (%):** Percentage of unemployed workforce[cite: 1]
* **Estimated Employed:** Total employed workforce count
* **Estimated Labour Participation Rate (%):** Total labor participation index

---

## 📈 Key Findings & Insights
1. **Pre-COVID Baseline:** National average unemployment oscillated between **8.8%** and **9.9%** from May 2019 to February 2020.
2. **COVID Lockdown Impact:** National unemployment surged dramatically to **24.88%** in May 2020 during peak nationwide lockdown restrictions[cite: 1].
3. **Urban vs. Rural Disparity:** 
   * **Urban areas** hit a peak unemployment rate of **~28.3%** in May 2020 due to heavy service sector and industrial shutdowns.
   * **Rural areas** experienced a lower peak of **~21.8%**, supported by continuous agricultural baseline activity.
4. **State-Level Extremes:**
   * **Highest Avg Unemployment:** Tripura (28.35%), Haryana (26.28%), Jharkhand (20.59%).
   * **Lowest Avg Unemployment:** Meghalaya (4.80%), Odisha (5.66%), Assam (6.43%).
